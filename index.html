Zifan (Eric) Zhao

I'm a PhD student at UIUC, working with Prof. Saurabh Gupta and Prof. Shenlong Wang. Previously I was a Research Assistant at NYU with Prof. Lerrel Pinto and Prof. Saining Xie. I graduated summa cum laude from NYU Shanghai, mentored by Prof. Keith Ross and Prof. Li Guo.

My research interest lies in robotics, multimodal representation learning and reinforcement learning.
